# Gaussian Curvature Modulus

The **Gaussian curvature modulus** (κ̄ or $\kappa_G$) is the elastic constant associated with [[Gaussian curvature]] in the [[Helfrich model]].

## Definition

Contribution to membrane energy:

$$F_G = \kappa_G \oint K \, dA$$

Where $K = c_1 c_2$ is the [[Gaussian curvature]].

## Gauss-Bonnet Theorem

For closed surfaces of fixed topology:

$$\oint K \, dA = 4\pi(1 - g)$$

Where $g$ is the genus (number of handles). This means:
- **Spherical vesicles** (g=0): $\oint K \, dA = 4\pi$
- The Gaussian term is constant for topology-preserving deformations

## Physical Significance

κ̄ becomes important for:
- **Topology changes** (fission, fusion)
- **Membrane edges** (pores, open membranes)
- **Non-spherical topologies** (tori, multi-compartment vesicles)

## Typical Values

Usually $\kappa_G \approx -\kappa$ to $0$ (negative for most lipids)
