# Active membrane deformations of a minimal synthetic cell

## Summary
Demonstrates that active microtubule networks inside DOPC GUVs drive membrane deformations one order of magnitude larger than passive thermal fluctuations. Reports characteristic deformation timescales of $2.5$-$10$ s and reduced volume fluctuations $\Delta R/R_0 \approx \pm 0.2$. Provides a DOPC bending rigidity measurement: $\kappa_{\text{eff}} = 13.4 \pm 2.5\,k_BT$ and identifies non-Gaussian deformation statistics at longer timescales — directly comparable to my observations of transient large deformations.

## Key Findings
- Fluctuation amplitude: $\sim 10\times$ above passive thermal baseline
- Characteristic timescales: $2.5$ s, $5$ s, and $10$ s depending on activity
- Reduced volume fluctuations: $\Delta R/R_0 \approx -0.2$ to $+0.2$ ($20\%$)
- DOPC bending rigidity: $\kappa_{\text{eff}} = 13.4 \pm 2.5\,k_BT$
- Non-Gaussian statistics at longer timescales (active signature)
- Membrane tension: $\sigma \approx 10^{-7}$ N/m
