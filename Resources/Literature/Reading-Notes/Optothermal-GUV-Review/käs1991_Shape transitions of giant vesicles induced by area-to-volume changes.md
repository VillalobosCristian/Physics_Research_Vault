# Shape transitions and shape stability of giant phospholipid vesicles in pure water

## Summary
Landmark experimental study documenting five types of shape transitions in DMPC/POPC GUVs driven by temperature-induced area-to-volume changes. Crucially demonstrates the **discocyte-stomatocyte (invagination)** transition pathway (Type 2) with $8\%$ volume loss between $36$-$27°$C. Provides the foundational experimental evidence for temperature-driven vesicle invaginations, directly comparable to my observations.

## Key Findings
- **Type 1 (Budding):** Sphere → pear → dumbbell → outside-budded shapes
- **Type 2 (Discocyte-Stomatocyte):** Progressive invagination with $8\%$ volume loss — MOST RELEVANT
- **Type 3 (Reentrant):** Dumbbell-pear-dumbbell transitions showing reversibility
- **Type 4 (Blebbing):** Spontaneous bleb and tether formation at critical excess areas
- **Type 5 (Complex):** Prolate → discocyte → stomatocyte with $6$-$20\%$ volume loss
- Thermal area expansivity: $\alpha = 7.3 \times 10^{-4}$ K$^{-1}$ for DMPC bilayer
- Critical temperature intervals for transitions: $\sim 0.1°$C width for Type 2
