# Directed tubule growth from giant unilamellar vesicles in a thermal gradient

## Summary
Shows that thermal gradients ($\nabla T \approx 0.1$ K/$\mu$m) applied across GUVs with DOPC/DPPC/cholesterol membranes induce directed lipid domain migration and tubule growth. Liquid-disordered (Ld) domains migrate toward the hot side, and above critical temperatures, tubules grow from the vesicle surface. Demonstrates thermal control over membrane structure without direct mechanical contact.

## Key Findings
- Thermal gradient of $0.1$ K/$\mu$m induces domain migration toward hot side
- Tubule growth rate depends on proximity to phase transition temperature $T_c$
- GUV size range: $5$-$35\,\mu$m diameter (electroformation)
- Below $T_c$: no spontaneous tubule growth; requires external heating
- Demonstrates thermal control over lipid membrane structure
