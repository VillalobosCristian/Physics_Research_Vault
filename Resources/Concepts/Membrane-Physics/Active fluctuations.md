# Active Fluctuations

**Active fluctuations** are non-equilibrium membrane undulations driven by energy-consuming processes, beyond thermal fluctuations.

## Distinction from Thermal Fluctuations

| Property | Thermal | Active |
|----------|---------|--------|
| Energy source | $k_BT$ | ATP, activity |
| Equilibrium | Yes | No |
| Detailed balance | Satisfied | Violated |
| Effective temperature | $T$ | $T_{\text{eff}} > T$ |

## Sources of Active Fluctuations

### Biological
- **Molecular motors** — Myosin, kinesin activity
- **Cytoskeleton dynamics** — Actin polymerization
- **Ion pumps** — Membrane protein activity
- **Metabolic activity** — ATP-driven processes

### Synthetic
- **Active colloids** — Self-propelled particles on/in vesicles
- **Bacterial baths** — Swimming microorganisms
- **Chemical reactions** — Marangoni-driven flows

## Signatures

Active fluctuations show:
1. **Enhanced amplitude** — Larger than thermal prediction
2. **Anomalous spectrum** — Different power-law scaling
3. **Broken time-reversal** — Asymmetric correlations
4. **Effective temperature** — $T_{\text{eff}} = T(1 + \text{activity})$

## Measurement

Modified fluctuation analysis:
$$\langle |u_n|^2 \rangle = \langle |u_n|^2 \rangle_{\text{thermal}} + \langle |u_n|^2 \rangle_{\text{active}}$$
