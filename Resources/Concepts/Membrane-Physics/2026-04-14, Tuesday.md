I'll start comparing observables between cycles, such as circularity first.
the minimum circularity during heatig, No fitting required, no normalization, And the second dip being smaller is a direct measurement of membrane stiffening or tension buildup between cycles.

I'll focus only in two heating cycles experimetns


| Symbol       | Definition          | Window                                                                                                             |
| ------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| $C_0$        | mean circularity    | Baseline 0                                                                                                         |
| $C_1^{\min}$ | minimum circularity | Heat 1                                                                                                             |
| $C_{ic}$     | mean circularity    | Inter-cycle (between Heat 1 and Heat 2) with this one i will use instead three points to see if there are recovery |
| $C_2^{\min}$ | minimum circularity | Heat 2                                                                                                             |
| $C_4$        | mean circularity    | Post-heat 2                                                                                                        |

$$\Delta C_1 = C_0 - C_1^{\min} \quad \text{(depth of first dip)}$$ $$\Delta C_2 = C_{ic} - C_2^{\min} \quad \text{(depth of second dip)}$$ $$\text{Recovery} = C_{ic} - C_0 \quad \text{(did circularity return to baseline?)}$$ $$\text{Ratio} = \frac{\Delta C_2}{\Delta C_1} \quad \text{(how much smaller is the second response?)}$$

If the membrane recovers during the inter-cycle window, the circularity rises from $C_1^{\min}$ back toward $C_0$, the mean collapses that trajectory into one number and you lose the recovery dynamics entirely.

Better to characterize the inter-cycle window with **three points** instead of one mean:

|Symbol|Definition|Physical meaning|
|---|---|---|
|$C_{ic}^{\text{start}}$|mean over first `window_fr` frames after transient|state just after cycle 1 ends|
|$C_{ic}^{\text{end}}$|mean over last `window_fr` frames before Heat 2|state just before cycle 2 starts|
|$C_{ic}^{\text{mid}}$|mean over middle `window_fr` frames|intermediate state|

Then recovery is directly:

$$\text{Recovery}_1 = C_{ic}^{\text{end}} - C_{ic}^{\text{start}}$$

positive = membrane recovered toward baseline during inter-cycle window, negative = continued deterioration.

And the full trajectory per experiment becomes:

$$C_0 \to C_1^{\min} \to C_{ic}^{\text{start}} \to C_{ic}^{\text{mid}} \to C_{ic}^{\text{end}} \to C_2^{\min} \to C_4$$

This maps directly onto what you see in the figure -- the rise from the first dip back toward the dotted threshold line is exactly $C_{ic}^{\text{start}} \to C_{ic}^{\text{end}}$.
