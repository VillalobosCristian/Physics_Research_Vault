# Rhodamine B Solution Protocol 

## 1: Buffer (20 mM, pH 9.6)

1. Dissolve 1 Sigma C3041 capsule in 40 mL water
2. Transfer to 100 mL volumetric flask, fill to mark
3. Verify pH 9.5-9.7
4. **Yields:** 100 mL buffer

## 2: Rhodamine Stock (10 mM)

1. Weigh 47.9 mg rhodamine B
2. Dissolve in 10 mL volumetric flask with water
3. Store at 4°C in dark (amber bottle or foil-wrapped)
4. **Yields:** 10 mL stock (stable 1-2 months)

## 3: Working Solution (0.1 mM)

1. Add 500 μL rhodamine stock to 50 mL volumetric flask
2. Fill to mark with buffer
3. Filter through 0.2 μm filter
4. Store at 4°C, protected from light
5. **Yields:** 50 mL working solution (use within 1-2 weeks)

---

- 10 mL stock makes ~20 batches of working solution
- Warm to room temp before use (30 min)
- Wear gloves, protect from light
- Solutions should be clear with no particles
