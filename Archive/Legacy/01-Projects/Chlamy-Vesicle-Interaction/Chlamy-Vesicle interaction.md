## Second round experiments

For the second test of experiments, I brought the algae from LOMA directly from the culture. Chlamy are in the culture medium TAP at approximately 21 mM. 
We want to mix the algae with the GUV which we produce at 200 mM in sucrose and then mixed with glucose at 200 mM for sedimentation, this will induce a osmotic shock to the algae. For this specific experiment I first mixed chlamy and glucose to reach 260 mM, which is the concentration of the produced GUV, the concentration of sucrose increase with evaporation in the hydration method, so is better to accommodate prior experiments. 
Chlamy in TAP were swimming normaly, and after putting in glucose at the desire concentration no swimming was visible. 
After 45 minutes most of the algae recover the swimming. I think this step can be done slower in order to avoid so huge osmotic change. 
Then algae were mixed with sucrose GUV and let the vesicle sediment, for the first test i put 200 uL of the algae in 100 uL of the sucrose GUV,  but the amount of good vesicle was very little.
For the next experiment,  I had the vesicle already sedimented for hours and take from the very bottom, and did the same mixing, this increase a lot the number of vesicles and proced to take some videos. 
For next experiments I would like to have more algae, so maybe a centrifugation step would be nice.
