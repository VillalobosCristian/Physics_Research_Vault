# Hydrodynamic manipulation of nano-objects by optically induced thermo-osmotic flows

## Summary
Demonstrates thermo-osmotic slip-driven hydrodynamic flows near a 50 nm gold film heated by 532 nm laser. Local temperature rise $\Delta T \sim 5$-$8$ K with flow velocities $10$-$100\,\mu$m/s. The slip coefficient $\chi \sim 10^{-10}$ m$^2$s$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$ quantifies the boundary-driven transport. Directly applicable to understanding flows near my Au/Cr substrate.

## Key Findings
- $\Delta T \sim 5$-$8$ K above heated spot on gold film
- Thermo-osmotic slip coefficient: $\chi \sim 10^{-10}$ m$^2$s$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$
- Flow velocities: $10$-$100\,\mu$m/s depending on power
- Temperature-modulated diffusion changes particle mobility
- Nanometer-scale positioning possible through force balance

## Relevance to My Research

### Direct Applications
- Same gold film + laser geometry as my setup
- $\Delta T$ values consistent with my calibrated temperature rise
- Thermo-osmotic flows explain the drift I observe during blue light illumination
- Flow velocities provide context for vesicle migration rates
