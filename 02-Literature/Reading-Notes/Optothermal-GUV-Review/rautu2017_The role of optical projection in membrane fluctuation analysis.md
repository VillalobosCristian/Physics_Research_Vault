# The role of optical projection in the analysis of membrane fluctuations

## Summary
Demonstrates that optical projection effects in microscopy systematically underestimate bending rigidity by $\sim 30\%$ if not corrected. For DOPC: uncorrected $\kappa = 19 \pm 1\,k_BT$ vs. corrected $\kappa = 27 \pm 1\,k_BT$. Develops a Bayesian correction framework accounting for focal depth. Critical methodological paper for anyone doing fluctuation spectroscopy with phase contrast.

## Key Findings
- Without correction: $\kappa = 19 \pm 1\,k_BT$
- With optical projection correction: $\kappa = 27 \pm 1\,k_BT$ (consistent with literature $28 \pm 3\,k_BT$)
- Correction factor: $\kappa_{\text{corrected}} \approx 1.4 \times \kappa_{\text{raw}}$ for typical phase contrast setups
- Focal depth $\pm 0.07\,\mu$m creates $30\%$ variation in measured $\kappa$
- Phase contrast focal depth: $\delta \approx 1$-$1.5\,\mu$m
