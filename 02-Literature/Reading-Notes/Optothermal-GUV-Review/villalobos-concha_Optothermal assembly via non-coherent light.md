# Optothermal assembly and manipulation of colloids via non-coherent light

## Summary
Demonstrates that standard microscope illumination (non-coherent LED) on gold film substrates generates sufficient optothermal effects for colloidal crystal assembly. Temperature rise $\Delta T \sim 5$-$8$ K. Thermo-osmotic slip is identified as the dominant particle confinement mechanism. Assembly is reversible upon light off. This is our own paper and establishes the experimental platform that I extend to GUVs.

## Key Findings
- Non-coherent white light sufficient for optothermal effects
- Temperature rise: $\Delta T \sim 5$-$8$ K (computational validation)
- Thermo-osmotic slip is dominant particle confinement mechanism
- Reversible assembly/disassembly upon light on/off
- Power density: $41.8$-$250$ kW/m$^2$ at sample
- Illumination zone radius: $75\,\mu$m (20×), $35\,\mu$m (40×), $25\,\mu$m (60×)
